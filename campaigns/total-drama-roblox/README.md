# Total Drama Roblox campaign (Vyro)

Source video: StinkyAsher, "I PLAYED IN THE FIRST EVER TOTAL DRAMA ROBLOX
TOURNAMENT", from the campaign's Frame.io link: https://f.io/7acN1ASv

1. Open the Frame.io link and download the MP4.
2. Run `Clipper.command`, pick this campaign, and drag the downloaded MP4 into
   the window (or run
   `python -m clipper "path/to/video.mp4" --campaign total-drama-roblox`).
3. You get 15 clips in `clips/<video>/TikTok`, `/Instagram` and `/YouTube`,
   plus `clips.txt` with the caption to paste for each one.

No logo: the brief bans logos, hashtags or watermarks that aren't part of the
campaign, so leave `logo.png` out unless the campaign gives you one.

## Rules to check before posting
- Caption must have **#TotalDramaRoblox** and **#paidpartnership** (already in
  `post.txt`). Also turn on the platform's paid-partnership / branded-content
  label if you can.
- At least **15 seconds** long (clips here are 20–50 s).
- TikTok videos, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels only. No Stories, carousels
  or collab posts; don't tag other accounts.
- Likes and comments **on**. Don't boost or promote the post.
- Nothing edited to misrepresent the video, Total Drama, Roblox or the
  creators. Skip any clip that needs context or is boring: low-quality posts
  are rejected.
- Each post needs 5,000 views to pay (max $1,000 per post). Never post the
  same clip twice on one account, and never use bought/botted views.
